Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 June 2026

1. Introduction

TradeHub ("we", "us") provides a mobile app for builders, tradespeople, clients, and inspectors to collaborate on construction projects, messaging, and invoicing. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide and data generated when you use the app:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, profile photo
  • Business information: company name, trade specialty, business address, tax identifiers, bank account details for invoicing
  • Project data: site addresses, tasks, documents, drawings, photos, progress updates, and inspections
  • Messaging: in-app chat messages and image attachments
  • Financial data: invoices, quotes, and payment records (method and amount — we do not collect full payment card numbers)
  • Location: precise location only when you use "Find a Contractor" (with your permission)
  • Identifiers: Firebase user ID and push notification token (FCM) for delivering notifications
  • Safety reports: content you submit when reporting chat or project issues

3. How we use information

We use your information to operate TradeHub: authentication, project collaboration, messaging, invoicing, notifications, address lookup, contractor search, and account management. We do not use your data for cross-app advertising or sell your data to data brokers.

4. Third-party services

We use trusted providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, file storage, cloud functions, push messaging
  • Google Places API — address autocomplete and contractor search near your location
  • LINZ (Land Information New Zealand) — property and parcel lookups
  • Auckland Council & Watercare — property and infrastructure data for Site Intelligence
  • Resend — sending transactional emails (e.g. invitations)
  • Apple — push notification delivery

These providers receive only the data needed to perform their function.

5. Data storage and security

Data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure. We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections and Firebase Authentication. Viewing full bank account numbers requires additional verification (step-up authentication).

6. Data retention and deletion

You may delete your account directly in the app via My Profile → ••• → Delete Account. Following account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days. Shared project records are anonymised (your name and personal details removed) and may remain visible to other project participants for record-keeping purposes.

7. Your rights

You may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. These rights may vary depending on your location. Contact us to exercise any of these rights and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Children

TradeHub is not intended for users under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Contact us

Email: support@tradehub.co.nz

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised version at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
