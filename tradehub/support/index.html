Account & onboarding

What's the difference between a Builder and other roles?

When you sign up, you choose how you work in TradeHub. Builder / Tradesperson covers people who build or trade — you'll pick a specialty and either set up your own business or join an employer. Client and Inspector are for people who view project updates or conduct inspections. Your onboarding choice shapes which features you see.

I work for a company — how do I join my employer on TradeHub?

During onboarding, choose that you're not managing your own projects, then enter the join code your employer shared. They approve your request from My Team. You can also skip that step and ask your employer to invite you by email from My Team instead.

What is business setup and do I have to complete it?

Business setup collects your trading name, business type, tax and registration details, address, and bank details. It's required before you can send invoices or quotes. Complete it during onboarding, from My Team, or Settings → Business.

What is a trade specialty and when do I set it?

Your specialty (e.g. plumbing, electrical) helps match you to the right work. Tradespeople set it during onboarding. It's also collected when you're invited to a company or project as a tradesperson.

My Team

What is My Team vs a project's Team?

My Team is your company on TradeHub — permanent staff, join codes, and your company calendar. A project's Team is who is on one job (builder, client, subcontractors, inspector). Company members are not automatically added to every project.

How do I invite team members?

There are two different flows. My Team (dashboard) is for your company — staff, join codes, and company calendar. To invite someone to your company, open My Team, tap +, and send an email invite. To add someone to a specific project, open the project, tap Team, then Add Team Member and enter their email and project role.

How do I invite someone to my company?

Open My Team from the dashboard, tap +, enter their email, choose their role (office staff or tradesperson), set whether they need financial access, and send the invite.

How do company join codes work?

As a company owner, open My Team and generate a join code. Share it with employees — they enter it during onboarding. You'll see their request under Pending join requests and can approve or decline. You can regenerate or revoke the code if it's been shared too widely.

How do I approve someone who requested to join my company?

Open My Team and review Pending join requests. Approve to add them — you can set their role, trade specialty, and financial access when approving.

Can I remove or edit a company member?

Yes. In My Team, tap a member to edit their role, financial access, or specialty. Owners can deactivate members who no longer work for the company. The company owner cannot be removed from the team list.

How do I edit my business name or details?

Open My Team and tap Edit on your business card, or go to Settings → Business. On that screen you can update your business name, address, logo, brand colour, GST, IRD/NZBN, and bank details. Business structure is shown but can't be changed in the app — use Contact support on that screen if you need it updated. Company type (Builder / Tradesperson / Client / Inspector) and your own trade specialties are set during onboarding; My Team shows company type but does not edit it. To change a team member's role, financial access, or specialty, open My Team and tap that member. To close the business, use Close my business at the bottom of Settings → Business.

I already own a business — can I also join another company?

Yes. A subcontractor can keep running their own business AND be added to a larger builder's company. Open My Team and tap "Join another company with a code". Enter the join code and submit — once they approve, you'll see both affiliations on My Team.

How do I leave a company I joined?

Open My Team, find the company you're a member of, and tap "Leave this team". Confirm in the alert — you'll lose access to that company's projects, invoices, and tasks. You can re-join later if the owner shares their join code with you again.

How do I close my business or switch to working for an employer?

Open Settings → Business and use Close my business at the bottom of the screen. This soft-closes your business: it hides it from your dashboard and stops you creating new invoices from it. Past invoices and quotes you've sent remain visible to recipients. There's no in-app way to reopen a closed business — contact support if you need help.

Projects

How do I add a new project?

Tap My Jobs from the dashboard, then tap the + button in the top right. Fill in the project details and tap Create Project.

How do I join a project using a code?

Open My Jobs, tap the key icon in the top right, enter the project code, preview the project, and submit your request. The project owner approves you and assigns your role.

What's the difference between joining a company and joining a project?

Joining a company links you to your employer's business on TradeHub. Joining a project adds you to a specific job site. You may need both — for example, be in your employer's company and separately invited onto individual projects.

Who can see project financials?

The Financials card on a project shows revenue, costs, and margin — and is only visible to the project owner (builder) on the project screen. Company members with Financial access can view and manage individual invoices and quotes via Billing, but they do not see the project-level Financials summary card.

How do I post a site notice?

Open a project, go to Notices, tap +, and write your update. Any team member on the project can post a notice. Use @Name to tag someone.

Can I reopen a completed or cancelled project?

When a project is marked Completed or Cancelled, it becomes a read-only archive. You can still view tasks, files, photos, billing, and messages, but nothing can be added or changed.

There is no in-app way to reopen a completed or cancelled project. If a project was closed by mistake, email support and include the project name — our team can restore it if appropriate.

Tasks & calendars

What's the difference between My Calendar, Company Calendar, and Project Calendar?

The dashboard calendar shows your personal tasks across projects, or your whole company's team schedule if you're the owner or work in admin/office. Project Calendar shows tasks for one job only.

How do I assign or delegate a task to a team member?

Open a task from a project, schedule it with start and end dates, and assign it to a team member from your company or project. The assignee sees it on their calendar and can accept or decline.

How do I create a task on a project?

Open a project, go to Tasks, tap +, fill in the details, and optionally schedule and assign it to someone.

Billing

How do I create an invoice or quote?

Open a project → Billing. Complete business setup first if prompted. Tap + to create an invoice or quote, add line items, set dates, and send.

Why can't I create an invoice yet?

You need a completed business profile and a default client on the project. TradeHub will prompt you to set these up before billing is unlocked.

What's the difference between an invoice and a quote?

A quote is a formal estimate before work is done. An invoice is a payment request after work or at a milestone. Both live under Billing on a project.

How do I record a payment on an invoice?

Open the invoice from Billing, tap Add Payment, enter the amount and date. The invoice status updates to reflect what's been paid.

How do I discuss an invoice or quote with the other party?

On the invoice or quote detail screen, tap Discuss this invoice or Discuss this quote. TradeHub opens a chat thread with the other party and document context attached.

Who can see my invoices on a project?

Visibility depends on your role on each document — generally the sender (supplier) and recipient (client) can see relevant invoices. Other project members may not see billing unless they're a party to that document.

Messaging & safety

Can I message team members?

Yes! Tap the message button (chat bubble) on any project to start a conversation with team members.

Can I message about a specific invoice without starting from scratch?

Yes — use Discuss this invoice or Discuss this quote on the document detail screen. It opens the right chat with context attached.

How do I report a user or issue?

From a chat or user profile, use Report. You can also email support@tradehub.co.nz or use Settings → Support in the app.

How do I block a user?

In any chat conversation, tap the user's profile, then select Block User. You can manage blocked users in Settings.

Inspections & documents

How do I schedule or book an inspection?

Open a project and tap Book Inspection. TradeHub opens Auckland Council's inspection booking site — you complete the booking there, not in the app. There is no in-app form to pick a date or inspection type.

How do I upload plans or engineering documents?

Open a project and go to Architectural Plans, Engineer Designs, or Designer Plans. Tap + to upload PDFs or images.

How do I upload site photos?

Open a project, tap Photos (or Files and Photos), then tap the + button. You can take a photo or choose from your library.

Notifications & account

How do I manage notifications?

Open Notifications from the dashboard bell icon. You'll see invites, task updates, join requests, and billing activity. Adjust preferences in Settings where available.

How do I update my business or profile details?

Settings → Business for company and trading details. Your profile name and account info are available from Settings and your user profile.
