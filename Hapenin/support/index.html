Common questions

I can't check in to a venue

Check-in confirms you're physically at the venue, so it needs location access and it needs you to be on site. If it isn't working:

  • Open your device's Settings → Hapenin → Location and set it to While Using the App, with Precise Location on.
  • Make sure you're inside or immediately outside the venue — GPS is less accurate indoors and in dense areas, so give it a few seconds to settle.
  • If the venue isn't listed at all, email us the name and address and we'll add it.

Nobody is showing at a venue near me

Hapenin only shows people who have checked in. Early on, and on quiet nights, venues will look empty because they are — the app isn't estimating or guessing crowd numbers.

My scan credits didn't arrive

Purchases occasionally stall between the app store and the app. Try Settings → Restore Purchases first. If credits are still missing, email us with the date of purchase and we'll sort it. Apple handles App Store refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, visit Google Play Help.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the ••• menu on any profile or message and choose Report or Block. Blocking is immediate and mutual — you disappear from each other entirely. We review every report and remove accounts that break our rules.

If you feel unsafe, contact local police first. In Australia, call 000.

Who can see my profile?

Only people you've accepted as Friends see your full profile. At a venue, others see limited signals without your name or photos. Friendship is always mutual — nobody can add you without your approval.

I'm not getting notifications

Check your device's Settings → Hapenin → Notifications is enabled, then Settings → Notifications in the app to confirm the specific types you want are on. Focus modes and battery saver can also delay delivery.

How do I change my email or password?

Settings → Account Details. If you're locked out, use Forgot Password on the sign-in screen, or email us.

Buy Me a Drink

Buy Me a Drink lets you send someone a $15 AUD drink while you're both checked in at the same venue. It's a real peer-to-peer payment — the modern equivalent of telling a waiter to send a drink to someone's table.

How it works

  • Both you and the other person must be checked in to the same venue at the same time.
  • Open their profile from the patron list and tap Buy them a drink.
  • The payment is a flat $15 AUD, processed instantly through Stripe.
  • The recipient is always shown who sent the drink — there are no anonymous drinks.
  • No message is attached to the drink.

Receiving drinks

To receive drinks from other patrons, you need to set up a payment account:

  1. Go to Settings → Payments → Set Up Payments.
  2. Complete the Stripe Express onboarding (bank details and identity verification).
  3. Make sure Accept Drinks is toggled on.

Once set up, the money from each drink lands in your connected bank account (minus a small platform and processing fee).

Opting out

If you don't want to receive drinks, go to Settings → Payments and turn off Accept Drinks. The "Buy them a drink" button will no longer appear on your profile. You can turn it back on at any time.

If you block someone, they can no longer see or send you drinks, and any future interaction between you is prevented.

Refunds and disputes

Buy Me a Drink payments are processed by Stripe, not through the App Store or Google Play. If you need to dispute a charge or request a refund, contact Stripe directly through the receipt email you receive after each payment, or email us at adrian.bucknell@gmail.com and we'll help resolve it.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Account Details → Delete Account.

This is permanent. Your account, profile, photos, check-in history, friend connections, messages, and drink history are removed within 30 days. Unused scan credits are forfeited. Any connected Stripe payment account is not deleted automatically — you can close it separately through Stripe. If you can't access the app to delete your account, email us from your registered address and we'll do it for you.
