Common questions

I can't check in to a venue

To check in, tap Check In, then choose Allow to show your presence at that venue to other people nearby. Choosing Not now cancels the check-in. Check-in also confirms you're physically at the venue, so it needs location access and you need to be on site. If it isn't working:

  • Open your device's Settings → Hapenin → Location and set it to While Using the App, with Precise Location on. Manual check-in never needs Always location.
  • Make sure you're inside or immediately outside the venue — GPS is less accurate indoors and in dense areas, so give it a few seconds to settle.
  • If several venues are close together, pick the right one from Where are you?
  • If the venue isn't listed at all, email us the name and address and we'll add it.

Auto Check-in

How does Auto Check-in work?

Auto Check-in is optional and off by default. When it's on, Hapenin checks you in when you arrive at a venue and checks you out when you leave, so you don't have to tap Check In yourself. You get a notification each time ("Checked in to …" / "Checked out of …"), so you always know your status.

Hapenin watches the 20 nearest venues within about 5 km of you using iOS geofencing. When you arrive within about 75 m of one, you're checked in. As you move to a new area, the list of nearby venues is updated. Hapenin does not continuously track your GPS location.

Turning Auto Check-in on

  1. Go to Settings → App Preferences and turn on Auto Check-in.
  2. Read the explainer and choose whether automatic check-ins show you on the map (Show me on the map).
  3. When iOS asks, allow location access Always. Hapenin only asks for Always location when you turn Auto Check-in on. If you don't allow it, Auto Check-in stays off and the rest of the app works as normal.
  4. Allow notifications so you're told about each automatic check-in and check-out.

Who sees my automatic check-ins?

With Show me on the map on, automatic check-ins work like manual ones: other people nearby see your presence at that venue, and your Friends may be notified. With it off, your automatic check-ins are hidden from other people at the venue and don't notify your Friends. You can change this at any time in Settings → App Preferences, and the new choice applies to future automatic check-ins.

Auto Check-in didn't check me in

  • Check that Settings → Hapenin → Location on your device is set to Always with Precise Location on. If you change it to anything other than Always, Hapenin turns Auto Check-in off. Turn it back on in the app when you're ready.
  • You need to be signed in. Signing out stops Auto Check-in until you sign back in.
  • If you're already checked in at another venue, Hapenin won't move you automatically. Check out first, or check in manually.
  • After an automatic check-in, Hapenin waits 30 minutes before automatically checking you in to the same venue again, so walking in and out doesn't check you in repeatedly.
  • iOS decides when to deliver arrival events, so they can take a few minutes, especially indoors. You can always check in manually instead.
  • The venue must be in Hapenin. If it isn't, email us the name and address.

Turning Auto Check-in off

Turn off Auto Check-in in Settings → App Preferences. Hapenin stops watching venues straight away and no further automatic check-ins happen. Setting location to While Using the App or Never in your device's Settings also turns it off. Manual check-in keeps working with While Using the App location.

More questions

Nobody is showing at a venue near me

Hapenin only shows people who have checked in. Early on, and on quiet nights, venues will look empty because they are — the app isn't estimating or guessing crowd numbers.

How do scans work?

A scan shows nearby venues and the mix of people checked in there. Everyone gets a free scan (5 km, limited preview) once every 7 days, and you can turn on a notification for when your next one is ready. Hapenin+ gives unlimited scans.

Hapenin+ and purchases

Hapenin+ is a monthly or annual subscription, billed by Apple. It includes unlimited scans, venues grouped by who's there, more venue likes, member and liker photos, and more than one filter when you host an event.

  • Subscribe or manage: Settings → Get Hapenin+. Once you're subscribed, the same row opens Manage subscription, where you can cancel through Apple.
  • Hapenin+ isn't showing after you paid: open the Hapenin+ screen and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you paid with. If it still isn't active, email us with the purchase date.
  • Refunds: Apple handles App Store refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, visit Google Play Help.

If you bought scan packs before Hapenin+ launched and something looks wrong with them, email us.

Venue likes

Tap the heart on a venue to like it. A like lasts 7 days. Free accounts can hold a few likes at once, and Hapenin+ can hold more. The map shows liked venues as a suburb pin, not the venue's exact location.

Your accepted Friends can see that you liked a venue, and may get a notification about it. People who aren't your Friends don't see your like. Hapenin+ Friends also see your profile photo and up to two interests or traits you share with them.

How do I report or block someone?

On a profile, tap the ••• menu and choose Report User or Block User. In venue chat, press and hold a message to Report Message or Block User. You can also report an individual profile photo. We review every report and remove accounts that break our rules.

Blocking takes effect immediately. A blocked person can't see your profile or activity, can't see events you host, and their venue chat messages are hidden from you. If you're both checked in at the same venue, you may still be in the same venue chat. Manage blocks in Settings → Privacy and Safety → Blocked Users. To keep someone as a Friend but hide what you're doing, use Hide Activity in the same section.

If you feel unsafe, contact local police first. In Australia, call 000.

Who can see my profile?

Only people you've accepted as Friends see your full profile. At a venue, others see limited signals — such as age band, interests, and your country if you've set it to Visible — without your name or photos. In venue chat, people see your username. Friendship is always mutual — nobody can add you without your approval.

There are some exceptions. If you host an event, people who can see it see your first name and profile photo. If you join an event, other members see your first name. Hapenin+ members also see your photo, and so does the host while you're waiting to be approved. Your gender is never shown on your profile. If you add a gender filter to an event you host, that filter is visible on the event.

I'm not getting notifications

Check your device's Settings → Hapenin → Notifications is enabled, then Settings → Notifications in the app to confirm the specific types you want are on (check-in, friend, venue, and free scan). Auto Check-in notifications come from your device rather than our servers, so they only need notifications allowed in your device's Settings. Focus modes and battery saver can also delay delivery. Notifications you've received are kept in the in-app inbox for 30 days.

How do I change my password or profile details?

Password: Settings → Account Details → Change Password. Name, photos, bio, interests, country, relationship status, living situation, and gender: Profile → Edit Profile. If you need to change your email address or you're locked out of your account, email us from your registered address and we'll help.

Events

Events let you get a group together at a venue. They show up on the area sheet when you open a suburb on the map.

Hosting an event

  • Pick a venue, give the event a name, an optional description, and a time. You can also choose decide later. An undated event expires after 7 days if no time is set.
  • You can host one open event at a time, dated up to 40 days ahead.
  • Choose public (shown to people who match your filters) or private (only you and approved members).
  • Choose Request to join (you approve each person) or Join immediately.
  • Filters can cover gender, age, relationship status, and living situation. Free accounts can use one filter category. Hapenin+ can combine them. You can only filter gender to your own.
  • You can edit the venue or time, remove members, end, or cancel the event. Members are notified of changes.

Joining an event

  • Open the event and tap to join or request. You'll be notified when the host approves you.
  • Approved members get the event chat and a join code. When you check in at the venue, use the code to join the event's group instead of the general venue chat.
  • You can leave an event at any time. Members get a reminder an hour before it starts.

I can't see an event a friend told me about

It's probably private, you don't match its filters, or one of you has blocked the other. Ask the host to check the event's settings.

Events, their chat, and memberships are deleted about 24 hours after the event ends or is cancelled.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Account Details → Delete Account.

This is permanent. Your account, profile, photos, identity details (including gender and country), check-in and scan history, friend connections, blocks, and messages are removed within 30 days. Events you host or joined follow the 24-hour event clean-up, and venue likes expire on their 7-day timer.

Deleting your account does not cancel Hapenin+ — cancel it in your Apple Account subscriptions first, or you'll keep being billed.

If you can't access the app to delete your account, email us from your registered address and we'll do it for you.
