# Hapenin Privacy Policy **Last updated:** 3 November 2026 · **Effective:** 3 November 2026 This policy explains what Hapenin collects, why, and what you can do about it. Hapenin is operated by **Bucknell Tech** ("we", "us"), **Queensland, Australia**. We are the data controller for the information described here. **Questions, requests, or complaints:** bucknelltech@gmail.com --- ## How Hapenin handles visibility Most of the app still uses three layers of disclosure. Events and venue likes add narrower exceptions, described below. **Anonymous layer.** At a venue, other users see demographic and interest signals only — for example age band, country of origin, relationship status, living situation, and interests. They do **not** see your real name, username, or profile photos. **Username layer.** In venue chat, and in an event's group chat, you appear under your chosen username. Your real name and profile photos remain hidden in those chats. **Friends layer.** Only **mutual Friends** — people you have both accepted — see your full profile, including your real name and profile photos. Friendship is always mutual. Nobody can follow you or view your full profile without your acceptance. You can block users or hide your activity from specific people in **Settings → Privacy and Safety**. Blocks and hidden-activity choices also apply to venue likes and event discovery. **Events.** A public event is shown to people who match the filters the host set. A private event is shown only to the host and approved members. People who can see an event see the host's **first name and profile photo**, the event name, description, venue, time, and a summary of its filters. They also see the **first names** of approved members. **Profile photos of members** are shown only to viewers with an active **Hapenin+** subscription. If the event asks people to request access, the host sees each requester's first name and profile photo so they can decide. Approved members share a join code used at the venue to enter that event's group instead of the venue chat. **Venue likes.** A like lasts 7 days. The map shows a suburb pin, not the venue's exact coordinate. The people who liked a venue — their first names — are visible to you and to your accepted Friends, not to everyone nearby. Hapenin+ subscribers in that Friends audience also see profile photos and up to two shared interests or traits. Friends can be notified when you like a venue or create an event they are allowed to see. **Gender stays private.** We store gender so events can be filtered, and we do not put it on your profile, venue cards, or chats. If you host an event and set a gender filter, that filter is shown on the event (for example "Male only"). Hosts can only filter gender to their own value, so people who can see the event can infer it. --- ## What we collect | Data | Why | Required? | |------|-----|-----------| | **Email address and password** | To create and secure your account. You turn it on in **Settings → App Preferences**, and you can turn it off at any time there or by changing location permission in device Settings. - **How it works.** Hapenin registers small geofences (about 75 m) around the nearest venues saved in Hapenin, using your device's region monitoring. When your device enters one, Hapenin takes a single precise location reading to confirm the nearest venue and checks you in. When you leave that venue's geofence, Hapenin checks you out. If you are already checked in, it does not change your check-in. - **What it does not do.** Hapenin does not continuously track your location or run GPS in the background. Geofence and location readings are used only to decide check-in and check-out, and are not stored as a location history. - **Map presence.** When you turn on Auto Check-in, you choose once whether automatic check-ins show you on the map (**Show me on the map**, on by default). That choice applies to every automatic check-in until you change it. When it is off, your automatic check-ins are hidden from other patrons at the venue and do not notify your Friends. - **Notifications.** Each automatic check-in or check-out posts a local notification on your device so you always know it happened. --- ## Identity profile and demographic data During onboarding you may optionally provide country of origin (up to 2). You must provide gender, relationship status, living situation, interests, and profile photos to use Hapenin. Country powers venue vibe matching when provided. Gender is used only for events. Relationship, living, interests, and photos are essential to the service. **Country visibility** - **Visible** — other users see your country on anonymous profile cards at venues (without your name, username, or photos). - **Hidden** — other users do not see your country on profile cards. Hapenin still uses your country to personalise venue matching for you. - You can skip country entirely, or change visibility and selections anytime from **Profile → Edit Profile**. **What others see** - At the **anonymous layer**, other users see your demographic signals (including country, relationship status, living situation, interests, and age band) as **aggregate-style profile cards** — without your name, username, photos, or gender. - At the **username layer** (venue chat and event chat), others see your username plus the same demographic signals in venue context — still without your real name or photos. - Only **mutual Friends** see your full profile. - **Events** and **venue likes** show first names, and in the cases in the visibility section, profile photos, to people who are not necessarily Friends. **How you control this data** - You can **update** your identity profile, gender, photos, and bio from your **Profile** at any time (tap **Edit Profile**). - You can **remove** individual identity selections (for example countries or interests) by editing your profile and saving the change. - You can **block** users or **hide your activity** from specific people in **Settings → Privacy and Safety**. - You can **delete your entire account** (see below), which removes your identity data, including gender. We explain what each identity screen collects and why at the point of collection during onboarding. The gender screen explains that gender is not shown on your profile. --- ## Events You can create one open event at a time, dated up to 40 days ahead, at a venue. You choose a name, an optional description, a time (or "decide later"), whether the event is public or private, and whether people request to join or join immediately. Filters can cover gender, age range, relationship status, and living situation. Free accounts can set one filter category. Hapenin+ can set more than one. People who do not match a filter do not see that public event. Private events stay with the host and approved members. Hosting, joining, requesting to join, and event chat are optional. Leaving, declining, removing a member, ending, or cancelling an event updates membership and notifies the people affected. Event records, memberships, the join code, event notifications, and event chat are deleted about **24 hours** after the event ends, is cancelled, or expires. --- ## Sensitive and special-category data Hapenin may collect **country / countries of origin** when you choose to provide it. Depending on your jurisdiction, country of origin may be treated as sensitive or special-category data. **Ethnicity**, if already stored on a profile, is also special-category data where the law treats racial or ethnic origin that way. **Gender** is collected as described above. We do not treat gender as a public profile field. Country is **optional**. If you add it, you choose **Visible** or **Hidden** on your profile: - **Hidden** means other users do not see your country on profile cards; we still use it for venue matching personalised to you. - **Visible** means other users see your country on anonymous profile cards (without your name or username). You can skip country, remove it, or change visibility anytime from your profile. Other demographic fields (gender, relationship status, living situation, interests) remain required for the service. Country is **never displayed against your real name or username** at the anonymous or username layer when shown. It contributes to demographic signals visible in venue scans and to match scoring only in anonymised form. On an event, a country or ethnicity filter the host chose appears as a filter summary, not as a label attached to each guest's name. If you do not want country processed, leave it blank or delete it from your profile. You can **delete your entire account** (see below), which removes all identity data. --- ## Our lawful bases | Basis | What it covers | |-------|----------------| | **Contract** | Account creation, required identity profile (gender, relationship, living, interests, photos), check-ins, venue scans, venue likes, events, Friends, venue and event chat, scan-pack purchases, and Hapenin+ while you are subscribed.
