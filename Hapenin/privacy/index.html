# Hapenin Privacy Policy **Last updated:** 6/08/2026 **Effective:** 6/08/2026 Hapenin is operated by **Adrian Bucknell**, Australia ("Hapenin", "we", "us"). This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and the choices available to you. **Privacy enquiries, requests, and complaints:** adrian.bucknell@gmail.com --- ## 1. How profile visibility works Hapenin uses different visibility levels. ### Venue profiles At venues, users may see profile information such as: - Age band - Relationship status - Living situation - Interests - Country of origin, if the user has selected **Visible** These profiles do not display the user's real name, username, or profile photos. ### Venue chat In venue chat, users are identified by their username. Real names and profile photos remain hidden. Country of origin is not displayed when the user has selected **Hidden**. ### Friends Friendship requires mutual acceptance. Friends may see a fuller profile, including real name and profile photos. A country marked **Hidden** remains hidden from other users, including Friends. Users can remove Friends, block users, and hide their activity from specific users. ### Buy Me a Drink When a user sends a drink, the recipient sees the **sender's first name**. This is not optional — all drink sends reveal who sent them. No message is attached to a drink. --- ## 2. Information we collect ### Account information Depending on how you register, we may collect: - Email address - Authentication credentials - Sign in with Apple or Google identifiers - First and last name - Username - Date of birth Passwords are managed by our authentication provider and are not stored by Hapenin in readable form. ### Profile information We may collect: - Up to three profile photos - An optional profile biography - Relationship status - Living situation - Interests - Optional country or countries of origin We do not ask users to provide gender. ### Country of origin Country of origin is optional. You can use Hapenin without providing it. If you provide country of origin: - It is used to personalise venue matching for you. - You can select **Visible** or **Hidden**. - **Visible** means it may appear on profile cards shown to other users. - **Hidden** means it is not displayed to other users, but Hapenin continues to use it to personalise venue matching for you. - You may remove it or withdraw your consent at any time. Removing country of origin stops it from being used in future country-based matching. ### Location information With your permission, we collect precise location while you use the app to: - Find nearby venues - Verify that you are physically present before checking in - Provide venue scans and matching - Determine when you leave a venue Background or "Always" location is optional and is used only if you enable Auto Check-in. You can change location permissions through your device's settings. ### Payment information **Scan-pack purchases** are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your complete payment-card details for these purchases. **Buy Me a Drink payments** are processed by **Stripe**. When you send or receive a drink payment: - We store a record of the transaction, including sender and recipient identifiers, the venue, the amount, and the Stripe payment intent identifier. - We do not store, process, or have access to your full payment-card number, expiry date, or CVV. This information is collected and processed directly by Stripe. - If you set up a Stripe Connect account to receive drink payments, we store your Stripe Connect account identifier to route payments to you. Stripe collects and processes your bank account details, identity verification documents, and other information required for its onboarding and regulatory obligations directly — Hapenin does not receive or store this information. ### Activity and social information We may collect: - Current and previous check-ins - Friend connections and requests - Venue chat messages - Drink transaction records (sender, recipient, venue, amount, status) - Blocked and restricted user lists - Content reports - Scan-pack purchases - Device push-notification tokens - Crash and usage diagnostics --- ## 3. Why we use your information We use personal information to: - Create and secure your account - Verify that users are at least 18 - Operate venue discovery and check-ins - Personalise venue matching - Display profile information according to your visibility choices - Deliver venue chat and Friend features - Process and record Buy Me a Drink payments between users - Reveal the sender's identity to the drink recipient - Send notifications you have enabled - Process scan-pack purchases - Investigate abuse and enforce our rules - Prevent fraud and protect account security - Diagnose faults and improve the service - Produce aggregated venue attendance and demographic statistics - Meet legal obligations We do not sell personal information. --- ## 4. Sensitive information and country of origin Depending on the applicable law and how it is used, country of origin may reveal or relate to racial or ethnic origin and may therefore be considered sensitive or special-category information. Providing country of origin is optional. Where required by law, including under the GDPR, we process country of origin only after you give explicit consent. This consent is separate from agreeing to our general Terms or Privacy Policy. You may: - Decline to provide country of origin - Keep it hidden from other users - Change its visibility - Remove it and withdraw consent Withdrawing consent does not affect your ability to use Hapenin's other features. It stops future country-based processing and matching after the information is removed. Selecting **Hidden** only controls display to other users. It does not withdraw consent to matching. To stop country-based matching, remove your country of origin or use the withdrawal control provided in your profile settings. --- ## 5. Lawful bases Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following lawful bases: ### Contract We process information necessary to: - Create and manage your account - Provide venue discovery and check-ins - Deliver venue chat and Friend features - Provide purchased scan packs - Process Buy Me a Drink payments, including recording transactions, routing payments via Stripe Connect, and revealing the sender's identity to the recipient ### Consent We rely on consent for: - Optional country-of-origin processing - Optional background location and Auto Check-in - Push notifications - Setting up a Stripe Connect account to receive drink payments (opt-in) - Other optional features where consent is requested Where country of origin constitutes special-category data, we rely on explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a), in addition to Article 6(1)(a). ### Legitimate interests We may rely on legitimate interests for: - Account and service security - Fraud prevention - Abuse investigations - Content moderation - Fault diagnosis - Improving reliability - Producing appropriately aggregated analytics We consider the effect of this processing on users' rights before relying on legitimate interests. ### Legal obligations We process or retain information where required by applicable law, including payment and financial record-keeping obligations. --- ## 6. Venue matching and analytics Country-based matching compares the country information you voluntarily provide with information associated with venue activity. If your country is **Hidden**, it may still be used to personalise venue rankings and matching results shown to you. It is not displayed on your profile to other users. We may provide venues with aggregated statistics, such as attendance totals or demographic distributions. We do not provide venues with: - Individual user profiles - Individual country selections - Names or usernames - Contact details - Payment or drink transaction details - Information intended to let a venue identify a particular user We apply aggregation safeguards designed to reduce the risk of identifying individual users. --- ## 7. Who receives your information We use service providers to operate Hapenin, including: - **Supabase** — authentication, databases, file storage, and server functions - **Stripe** — payment processing for Buy Me a Drink, including Stripe Connect onboarding, identity verification, bank account details, and payment routing - **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** — push notifications - **Google** — Google Sign-in and Places services - **Apple** — App Store distribution, Sign in with Apple, and in-app purchases These providers process information according to their own privacy terms and their agreements with us. Stripe processes payment-card details, bank account information, and identity verification documents directly. Hapenin does not receive or store this information. Stripe's privacy policy is available at [stripe.com/privacy](https://stripe.com/privacy). We may also disclose information: - When required by law - To protect users or the public from serious harm - To investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents - As part of a business sale or reorganisation, subject to appropriate safeguards --- ## 8. International transfers Hapenin is operated from Australia. Our providers may process information in Australia, the United States, the European Economic Area, or other jurisdictions. Where European or UK law requires transfer safeguards, we use recognised mechanisms such as Standard Contractual Clauses or other legally approved safeguards. You may request further information by contacting adrian.bucknell@gmail.com. --- ## 9. Retention We retain information only for as long as reasonably required for the purposes described in this policy. Typical retention periods are: - **Account and profile:** while the account remains active - **Country of origin:** until removed, consent is withdrawn, or the account is deleted - **Active check-ins:** until checkout, departure, or automatic expiry - **Check-in history:** while the account remains active, unless deleted sooner - **Venue chat messages:** up to 24 hours - **Drink transaction records:** while the account remains active, or as required for financial record-keeping obligations - **Stripe Connect account identifier:** while the account remains active or until the user disconnects their payment account - **Content reports:** up to 12 months after resolution - **Purchase records:** up to 7 years for financial and tax record-keeping - **Diagnostics:** up to 12 months - **Deleted accounts:** personal information removed or de-identified within 30 days, unless longer retention is legally required Aggregated information that can no longer reasonably be associated with an individual may be retained longer. --- ## 10. Account deletion You can delete your account in: **Settings → Account Details → Delete Account** Account deletion permanently removes or de-identifies your: - Profile - Identity information - Country selections - Photos - Friend connections - Check-in information - Messages - Drink transaction records (sender and recipient identifiers are removed or de-identified) Deletion is completed within 30 days, except where information must be retained by law (for example, financial transaction records may be retained in anonymised form for tax and regulatory purposes). Unused scan credits are forfeited. Scan-pack refund requests are handled according to Apple's App Store or Google Play policies. Your Stripe Connect account is not automatically deleted when you delete your Hapenin account. You can close your Stripe account separately through Stripe's dashboard or by contacting Stripe support. --- ## 11. Your choices and rights You can: - Edit your profile information - Skip or remove country of origin - Change country visibility - Withdraw country-processing consent - Change location permissions - Disable notifications - Enable or disable receiving drink payments - Block or restrict users - Delete your account Depending on your location, you may also have rights to: - Access your personal information - Correct inaccurate information - Request deletion - Restrict processing - Object to processing - Receive portable information - Withdraw consent - Complain to a privacy regulator Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that lawfully occurred before withdrawal. Submit requests to **adrian.bucknell@gmail.com**. EEA and UK requests will normally receive a response within one month. Australian users may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at [oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au). --- ## 12. Where legally required, we will notify affected users and relevant regulators of qualifying data breaches. --- ## 14. Changes to this policy We may update this policy as Hapenin changes.
